Core Concept: Open Component Lens v3.1 Pro
Open Component Lens is a secure, serverless, single-file hardware lab assistant and electronic component inventory manager designed for engineers, makers, and electronics hobbyists. It bridges physical component storage with digital project tracking and AI-powered datasheet extraction.

1. Secure Client-Side Architecture & Cloud Sync
Zero-Knowledge Privacy: Uses AES-256 encryption and a master PIN system so sensitive API keys and component inventories remain completely secure in the browser.

Cloud Persistence: Automatically synchronizes inventory and project states directly to a private cloud container via JSONBin.

2. AI Vision Inventory Management (Pillar 1)
Component Vault: A structured cataloging system that categorizes hardware parts (Microcontrollers, Power Regulators, Passives, Sensors) with real-time stock quantity controls (+ / -).

AI Datasheet Parser: Features a camera scan and image upload pipeline powered by Gemini 1.5 Flash to automatically extract part numbers, voltage thresholds, and descriptions from physical hardware or packaging.

Shortage Detection: Instantly flags components running low on stock or tied up in active builds.

3. Integrated Project Tracker & Playground (Pillar 2)
Build Binding: Links specific physical components from your inventory vault directly to active hardware projects (e.g., binding an ESP32 and LM317 to an "IoT Weather Station" project).

Lab Workspace: Provides a centralized hub to monitor active builds, manage project requirements, and track what hardware is allocated versus what is available in free stock.

Based on the current live application interface of your Open Component Lens app, here is how the AI Lens Component Scanner (camera snap / image upload) works, along with the complete remaining UI layout breakdown so you have everything documented for Google Stitch:

1. How the AI Lens Component Scanner Works (Camera Snap vs. Gallery Upload)
The component scanner popup modal handles two distinct methods of ingesting hardware data for Gemini 1.5 Flash to analyze:

Method A: Live Webcam Stream ([📹 Camera] & [🎯 Snap Photo])

Step 1: Clicking the [📹 Camera] button activates the browser's navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia API, streaming live video from your device camera into a video element inside the scanner modal.

Step 2: Once you align the physical microchip or component packaging in front of the lens, clicking [🎯 Snap Photo] freezes the video frame and draws it onto an internal HTML canvas.

Step 3: The canvas converts the image frame into a Base64 encoded JPEG data URL.

Method B: Gallery Upload ([📤 Upload])

Step 1: Clicking [📤 Upload] opens your local operating system file explorer.

Step 2: Selecting a local photo of a component reads the file via the JavaScript FileReader API and converts it into a Base64 encoded JPEG buffer.

Step C: AI Extraction Pipeline ([✨ Run Gemini AI Lens])

Step 1: Once the image is captured or uploaded, clicking [✨ Run Gemini AI Lens] packages the Base64 image and a strict extraction prompt and sends it to the Gemini 1.5 Flash API.

Step 2: Gemini parses the IC text, part markings, and physical package type, automatically auto-filling the form fields below (Component Title, Part Number, Category Dropdown, Min/Max Voltage, and Datasheet Summary).

Step 3: Clicking [Save Component] pushes the parsed data directly into your local and cloud-synced inventory vault.
